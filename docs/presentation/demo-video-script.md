# RetroMind AI — Demo Video Script

**Duration:** 3-4 minutes
**Tool:** Any screen recorder (OBS Studio, QuickTime Player, macOS built-in)

---

## Scene 1: Opening (15s)

**Visual:** Browser opens to login page
**Narrator:**
> "RetroMind AI — making EV retrofits safer, faster, and smarter. We help workshops convert imperfect vehicles into EVs with AI-powered engineering intelligence."

**On-screen:** Title overlay "RetroMind AI — EV Retrofit Intelligence"

---

## Scene 2: Authentication (15s)

**Visual:** Click "Use Demo Key" → redirected to assessment workspace
**Narrator:**
> "One click to start. No signup friction — workshops get immediate value."

---

## Scene 3: Photo Upload (30s)

**Visual:** Drag-and-drop 3 vehicle photos into upload slots (left, right, rear)
**Narrator:**
> "Upload 3 mandatory views: left profile, right profile, and rear. The system validates quality — blur detection, swap detection, occlusion checks. Optional views add precision."

**On-screen:** Highlight quality check badges (green checks)

---

## Scene 4: Assessment Pipeline (40s)

**Visual:** Click "Start Assessment" → progress stages animate in real-time
**Narrator:**
> "Click start. In under 60 seconds, 11 AI stages run: classification, geometry extraction, deviation detection, feasibility scoring, risk analysis, battery optimization, wiring guidance, and more. Each stage is visible as it completes."

**On-screen:** Progress bar filling, stage names lighting up

---

## Scene 5: Results & Confidence (30s)

**Visual:** Assessment result page shows confidence score, feasibility label, vehicle type
**Narrator:**
> "Results show confidence score (0-100), feasibility label, and detailed factor breakdown. The system explains *why* it reached each conclusion — no black box."

**On-screen:** Highlight confidence gauge, factor bars, vehicle classification

---

## Scene 6: Deviation Detection (30s)

**Visual:** Scroll to deviation summary — anomalies listed with severity
**Narrator:**
> "The deviation engine detects frame asymmetry, weld modifications, structural damage — things static tools miss. Each finding maps to a risk record."

**On-screen:** Highlight deviation list with severity badges

---

## Scene 7: Adaptive Recommendations (30s)

**Visual:** Battery placement section shows "Standard" vs "Adapted" layout
**Narrator:**
> "This is our core differentiator: deviation-aware recommendations. When structural issues are found, battery placement adapts automatically — with explanation of why and what the risk would be with the standard layout."

**On-screen:** Highlight "Adaptation Reason" text and the comparison

---

## Scene 8: Compliance Report (20s)

**Visual:** 13-section report page
**Narrator:**
> "The 13-section compliance report covers everything: assessment metadata, risks, battery placement, wiring, cost estimation, tooling, digital twin, and infrastructure health."

**On-screen:** Scroll through report sections

---

## Scene 9: CAD Export (15s)

**Visual:** Download STEP file → open in viewer
**Narrator:**
> "Export STEP or STL 3D models via the FreeCAD worker — directly from assessment geometry data."

---

## Scene 10: Knowledge Graph (15s)

**Visual:** Neo4j knowledge graph visualization
**Narrator:**
> "Every retrofit enriches the Retrofit DNA knowledge graph. Similar past jobs influence future recommendations — the system learns with use."

---

## Scene 11: Closing (15s)

**Visual:** Architecture diagram or system overview
**Narrator:**
> "RetroMind AI — deployed on Oracle Cloud Free Tier, built for ARM64, production-grade from day one. Making every retrofit safer, faster, and smarter."

**On-screen:** GitHub URL, tech stack logos, "Thank You"

---

## Production Notes

| Element | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Resolution** | 1920x1080 (1080p) |
| **Frame rate** | 30fps |
| **Audio** | Clear voiceover, no background music |
| **Captions** | Optional — burned-in subtitles recommended |
| **Transitions** | Simple cuts, no fancy transitions |
| **Cursor** | Visible and smooth movement |
| **Screenshot fallback** | If live app recording fails, use screenshots from `screenshots/` with Ken Burns effect |
